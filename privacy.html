Tremors – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app Tremors (package name com.peak_resources.tremors.shakyhands), published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us"). It explains what the app does with information.

The short version

Tremors does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything the app records stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools.

Sensor data

While you run a measurement, Tremors reads your phone's motion sensors (linear acceleration and gyroscope) to estimate how steady your hand is. This sensor data is processed on your device in real time to draw the live graph and calculate your score. The raw sensor data is not uploaded or shared. The app does not access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

Data saved on your device

If you choose to save a measurement, the result (such as the score, date, and duration) is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete any measurement within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data.

Your device's operating system may include app data in its own device backups (for example, Android's backup to your Google account), depending on your device settings. That backup is managed by your device and your account provider, not by us.

Sharing of information

We do not share any information with third parties, because we do not receive any.

Children's privacy

Tremors does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Tremors is intended for entertainment and general curiosity. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any medical condition. If you are concerned about hand tremors, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and revise the effective date above.
